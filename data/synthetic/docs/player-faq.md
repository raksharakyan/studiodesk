# Player FAQ

Answers to the questions players ask Starfall Outpost support most often. This FAQ covers the game up to version 1.4.2 (Storm Season).

## What is Starfall Outpost?

Starfall Outpost is a co-op survival and base-building game set on a chain of frontier moons. You land with a cryo pod, an oxygen reactor and not much else, then expand into a working outpost: hydroponic farms, fusion reactors, rovers and research. You can play solo or in co-op with up to 8 players (4 players before version 1.3.0).

The game is available on PC (Steam, including Steam Deck), PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch. All platforms receive the same content updates on the same day.

## Which content updates have been released?

- **1.1.0 – The Frozen Belt:** a new ice moon, thermal suits and the ORB-7 companion drone.
- **1.2.0 – Deep Core:** mining lasers, Tier 2 fusion reactors and cross-save between platforms.
- **1.3.0 – Outpost Alliance:** 8-player co-op, trade terminals and shared research.
- **1.4.0 – Storm Season:** dynamic lightning storms, lightning rods, storm shutters and wind turbines.

Smaller hotfixes (1.0.1, 1.1.1, 1.2.1, 1.3.1, 1.4.1, 1.4.2) are listed in the in-game news panel and in the patch notes.

## How does saving work?

The game autosaves when you sleep in the cryo pod, when you leave a moon and every 15 minutes. A spinning saving icon appears in the bottom-right corner while a save is being written. Since version 1.0.1 cryo-sleep autosaves are written atomically and the previous save is kept as a backup, so an interrupted save can no longer corrupt both copies.

Please wait for the saving icon to disappear before closing the game, suspending the console (PS5 rest mode, Xbox Quick Resume, Switch sleep) or turning off your PC.

## I lost progress after sleeping in the cryo pod. Can it be recovered?

Often, yes. Open **Load Game**, highlight the affected slot and press **Restore Backup**. The backup is the last save that was fully written before the cryo autosave. If the restore option is greyed out, contact support with your platform, game version and the time the problem happened; do not delete the slot.

## How do I unlock trophies and achievements that did not pop?

Some trophies were fixed in later patches: *Cold Feet* (fixed in 1.3.1) and *Eye of the Storm* (fixed in 1.4.1). If you met the requirement before the fix, repeat the final step once on the current version and the trophy will unlock. Trophies cannot be granted manually by support.

## Does Starfall Outpost support controllers, colourblind modes and subtitles?

Yes. Every platform supports a gamepad; PC also supports mouse and keyboard and switches prompts automatically. Accessibility options include three colourblind palettes (power wires follow the palette since 1.4.0), subtitle size and background, Celsius or Fahrenheit units for the thermal suit gauge, aim assist strength and hold-versus-toggle for all actions.

## How do I report a bug?

Use **Menu → Support → Report a Bug** in game. It attaches your platform, game version and a recent log automatically. Please describe what you expected to happen, what actually happened and the steps that lead to it. Our QA team reviews every report; duplicates are merged into the original ticket, so you may be pointed to an existing report.
