# Platform and System Requirements

Supported platforms, hardware requirements and platform-specific notes for Starfall Outpost 1.4.2.

## Supported platforms

| Platform | Store | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| PC | Steam | Windows 10/11 64-bit; Steam Deck Verified |
| PlayStation 5 | PlayStation Store | Performance and Quality modes, DualSense adaptive triggers |
| Xbox Series X\|S | Microsoft Store, Game Pass | Quick Resume supported; Series S runs at 1080p |
| Nintendo Switch | Nintendo eShop | Docked 900p, handheld 720p, dynamic resolution |

PlayStation 4, Xbox One and macOS are not supported.

## PC minimum requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10 64-bit (version 22H2 or later)
- **CPU:** Intel Core i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 2600
- **Memory:** 12 GB RAM
- **GPU:** NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6 GB or AMD Radeon RX 580 8 GB
- **DirectX:** version 12
- **Storage:** 35 GB available space, SSD strongly recommended
- **Network:** broadband connection for online co-op

Minimum targets 1080p at 30 fps on the Low preset.

## PC recommended requirements

- **OS:** Windows 11 64-bit
- **CPU:** Intel Core i7-10700 or AMD Ryzen 7 3700X
- **Memory:** 16 GB RAM
- **GPU:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT
- **Storage:** 35 GB available space on an SSD

Recommended targets 1440p at 60 fps on the High preset. Hosting 8-player sessions benefits from 8 or more CPU cores. HDR requires a display with at least 600 nits peak brightness; use **Settings → Display → HDR Calibration** (range improved in 1.3.0).

## Console performance modes

- **PS5:** Performance mode targets 60 fps at dynamic 1440p; Quality mode targets 30 fps at 4K. HDR is supported in both modes.
- **Xbox Series X:** same modes as PS5. **Xbox Series S:** a single mode targeting 60 fps at dynamic 1080p.
- **Nintendo Switch:** 30 fps target; the Deep Core and storms may use reduced effects to keep frame rate stable.

## Network requirements for co-op

Online co-op needs the platform's online subscription on consoles (PlayStation Plus, Xbox Game Pass Core or Ultimate, Nintendo Switch Online). PC requires no subscription. The host's upload bandwidth should be at least 5 Mbps for 4 players and 10 Mbps for 8 players. Matchmaking prefers the nearest region automatically since 1.2.1.

## Storage and save data

Install size is about 35 GB on PC and consoles and 14 GB on Switch. Each save slot takes 20–60 MB depending on the size of your outpost. Cloud saves are handled by each platform's own cloud storage; cross-save between platforms is a separate feature that uses your Starfall ID (see the co-op and cross-save guide).
