StudioDesk — AI support & QA agent for game studios

StudioDesk answers player and QA questions with cited sources, spots duplicate bug reports before they reach your tracker, and drafts GitHub issues that are filed only after a person presses Confirm. It searches the studio's own bug reports, crash logs, patch notes and support docs, and you can use it by typing or by talking to it.

Demo data: the game Starfall Outpost is fictional. The knowledge base holds 66 bug reports, 25 crash logs, 10 patch notes and 4 support docs, all made up for this demo.

            Where do issues go?
            
      
            
        When you press Confirm in the live app, StudioDesk files the issue on GitHub in a public
        sandbox repository, raksharakyan/starfall-outpost-issues,
        so you can see the result. A Slack alert may be sent as well.
      
            
      
            Nothing is ever filed without a click on Confirm and file issue; the voice agent cannot confirm. In this recorded demo nothing is filed at all, and the live app keeps daily limits on issue filing and voice calls.
            
    
            

    
            
      
            Talk to StudioDesk
            
      
            
        In the live app you can talk to StudioDesk through your microphone (ElevenLabs Agents).
        It can answer questions and check bug reports by voice, but issues are never filed by
        voice: you confirm them on the page. This recorded demo plays no audio and never uses
        your microphone.
